The Mission

Care is the mission. Pyre is how something new gets built from what crisis burns down.

Legacy systems manage the symptoms of poverty and crisis while extracting whatever resources are left in the process. CarePyre exists to do the opposite: build real, owned infrastructure — connectivity, identity, guidance, and protection — directly with the people who need it, so that a crisis becomes the ignition point for a sovereign, self-determined life instead of a debt someone else profits from.

"The Pyre is the holy fire from which something is reborn out of its own ashes — not destroyed by the crisis, built by it."
The C.H.A.N.G.E. Stack

Four layers, built to stay with you when everything else gets cut off.

Most support systems fail at the exact moment someone needs them most — a phone gets shut off, an email account gets abandoned, a form goes unanswered. CarePyre's stack is designed around that specific failure point.

LAYER 1

Community Telecom Mesh

Local, un-cancellable wireless connectivity — so losing a phone bill never means losing contact with the world.

LAYER 2

Autonomous Identity

A permanent digital identity and inbox that stays yours — not tied to a job, a carrier, or a bank account that can disappear.

LAYER 3

Human-Centered AI Navigation

Local AI tools that help people understand leases, benefits, and paperwork in plain language — real navigation, not a hotline.

LAYER 4

Sovereign Finance & Life Protection

Real financial protection and estate planning tools — so the gains one generation builds actually reach the next one.

How It's Built

Owned by the people it serves, not extracted from them.

CarePyre is organized as a holding trust with two arms — a nonprofit foundation and a worker-owned cooperative — so that value created locally stays local.

501(c)(3) Non-Profit

CarePyre Foundation

  • Grants and philanthropic funding
  • Direct human services and shelter support
  • Community education cohorts
  • Community board oversight
Worker-Owned Cooperative

CarePyre Networks

  • Builds and maintains the local mesh network
  • Runs the identity and email infrastructure
  • Local tech jobs at real, living wages
  • Reinvests surplus into the community, not shareholders
