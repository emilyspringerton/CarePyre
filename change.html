The Framework

Community Connectivity, Human-Centered AI, Autonomous Identity, Network Sovereignty, Grassroots Financial Growth, and Economic Independence.

C.H.A.N.G.E. is a six-pillar technology and workforce framework designed to move social service delivery from managing poverty to building permanent economic sovereignty — built with the people it serves, and alongside the community organizations, congregations, and nonprofits already doing this work every day.

"A crisis can take everything at once — a phone, a stable address, a support network — faster than any single organization can replace it alone. C.H.A.N.G.E. is the infrastructure underneath the response."
The Six Pillars

What each letter actually means.

C
Physical Layer

Community Connectivity

Local, community-owned wireless infrastructure on the roofs of shelters and partner sites — so losing a phone bill never means losing contact with the world. Zero-cost, un-cancellable connectivity for the people who need it most, built alongside the organizations already serving them.

H
Cognitive Layer

Human-Centered AI

Real tools, not a hotline: local AI that helps people read a dense lease, understand a housing voucher, or push back on a predatory fee in plain language — a cognitive exoskeleton for navigating systems that were never built to be navigated alone.

A
Passport Layer

Autonomous Identity

A permanent phone number and digital identity that stays yours — not tied to a job, a carrier, or a bank account that can vanish overnight. Real, durable access to 2FA, email, housing portals, and employment systems, no matter what else changes.

N
Infrastructure Layer

Network Sovereignty

The network the community builds is owned by the community — not rented from a carrier, not an ongoing expense. Over time, it becomes a self-funding asset instead of a recurring bill.

G
Monetary Layer

Grassroots Financial Growth

Real financial education and tools that let income earned through new skills and AI-augmented work actually stay with the person who earned it — protected from predatory fees and the instability of being unbanked.

E
The Outcome

Economic Independence

A real, non-exploitative career pipeline — from a supported classroom stipend, to a paid apprenticeship, to becoming a worker-owner earning a real living wage. The point of every other pillar: a permanent exit from crisis, not a longer stay inside it.
